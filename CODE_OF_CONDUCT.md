# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in Agent Baton to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size,
disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the project and the community.
- Keep discussion technical and on topic.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, or discriminatory language or imagery.
- Public or private harassment, and publishing others' private information without permission.
- Spam, or any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and when representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

The maintainer's handling, confidentiality and review responsibilities are also described in the [governance policy](docs/GOVERNANCE.md). A reporter may request a second, independent review if the complaint involves the maintainer; do not post confidential allegations publicly.

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and other contributions that violate this code, and may
temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behaviour they judge inappropriate. Report problems privately through
GitHub's private reporting on the repository (see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)) or to the maintainer,
[@BhurkeSiddhesh](https://github.com/BhurkeSiddhesh). Reports are handled confidentially.
